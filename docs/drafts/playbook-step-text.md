## Run an event (any size): 4 steps

### File the Event Proposal (DocuSign) · `proposal` · 14 days before

Every event needs an approved Event Proposal, filed by an Authorized
Representative: [Event Proposal form](https://na2.docusign.net/Member/PowerFormSigning.aspx?PowerFormId=649e3cb3-3650-479f-a4b9-bf1ae2d7e7de&env=na2&acct=abcfecea-7d7c-4b44-88dc-4279ba599ef8&v=2)

Work out your size first. It sets the deadline:

- **Small** (up to 50 people, $700): at least 5 business days ahead, 10 recommended
- **Mid** (up to 100 people, $1,400): at least 2 weeks ahead, 4 recommended
- **Large** (up to 250 people): 6 weeks ahead
- **Major** (over 250, or advertised to the public): 8 weeks ahead

Serving food? Say so in the proposal. Need a tent or AV equipment? Plan those now:

- Tent: https://forms.gle/LWTZSTPW5xryuFXc7
- AV equipment: Learning Technologies, 2 weeks ahead (https://its.ucsc.edu/av-checkout)

On the day, keep an attendance log with names and emails.

### Big event: Event Proposal due (6 weeks; 8 if over 250 or public) · `proposal-big` · 42 days before

Large and major events need the Event Proposal in much earlier than small ones:

- **Large** (up to 250 people): 6 weeks ahead
- **Major** (over 250, or advertised to the public): 8 weeks ahead. Move this step to 56 days if that's you.

An Authorized Representative files it: [Event Proposal form](https://na2.docusign.net/Member/PowerFormSigning.aspx?PowerFormId=649e3cb3-3650-479f-a4b9-bf1ae2d7e7de&env=na2&acct=abcfecea-7d7c-4b44-88dc-4279ba599ef8&v=2)

Students can't sign contracts.

### Meet an advisor (Peer Advisor drop-in; SOMeCA for mid-size and up) · `advisor` · 7 days before

Once the Event Proposal is in, meet an advisor:

- **Small events** (up to 50 people): a Peer Advisor drop-in is enough.
- **Mid, large and major events:** book a SOMeCA advisor meeting.

Bring the questions the rules raise:

- Selling food at a fundraiser? It must be store-bought and pre-packaged, and it needs a food permit through your advisor.
- Planning a prize drawing? Raffles are illegal; free-entry drawings are OK.

### Off-campus: meet the advisor (no official off-campus events) · `offcampus` · 35 days before

UCSC has no official off-campus events. Talk it through with your advisor
before you plan anything.

If members go somewhere off campus anyway, every post about it must say:
"This is not a UCSC-sponsored activity."

Students can't sign contracts.

## Promote the event: 3 steps

### Design the flyer (with the access line) · `flyer` · 21 days before

Design the flyer 3 to 4 weeks before the event. Every flyer needs:

- the org name
- a removal date
- the access line: "If you have disability-related needs, contact [name] at [email] as soon as possible."

Printed flyers can be at most 17×22".

### Post on Instagram, WhatsApp and the site · `promo-week` · 14 days before

Two weeks before, post the flyer:

- Instagram
- WhatsApp
- the site calendar
- printed flyers on designated boards only, following each college's posting rules

No chalking. Banners need approval. Quarry Plaza tabling is closed until Fall
2027; to table at the library, use the [library tabling form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSeRImnhgI5NPfOR90zXyxijX6x_pgHcaj_PSP0PBpD7hzG1gg/viewform).

### Post a reminder · `promo-eve` · 1 day before

Post a reminder the day before the event.

## Optional: Buy food, 2 steps

### New vendor? Get the 204 payee setup · `vendor-204` · 21 days before

Paying a restaurant or vendor the university hasn't paid before? It needs a
**204 Payee Setup** first, which takes 10 business days:
[204 Payee Setup](https://financial.ucsc.edu/Pages/WF_Payee_Setup_204.aspx)

Full catering must be UCSC Catering or an approved caterer. Restaurant takeout
is fine. Ask UCSC Catering about halal before ordering.

### Submit the Purchase Request (DocuSign) · `purchase` · 10 days before

Your approved Event Proposal must say you'll serve food. Then submit the
**Purchase Request** at least 5 business days ahead, or 10 for services:
[Purchase Request form](https://na2.docusign.net/Member/PowerFormSigning.aspx?PowerFormId=72406750-4802-4826-a97f-81bf1b2b6dca&env=na2&acct=abcfecea-7d7c-4b44-88dc-4279ba599ef8&v=2)

You get a PO and the university pays the vendor. Keep the original itemized
receipt.

**Paid yourself?** File the reimbursement within 2 weeks (4 at most) with the
original itemized receipt and proof of payment. A different Authorized
Representative submits it. Gift cards, alcohol, gas and services are never
reimbursed. Pay speakers by PO beforehand. No self-serve buffets.
